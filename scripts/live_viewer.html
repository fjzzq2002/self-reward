Self-reward: random-reward controls, live

Qwen3.5-2B, no thinking (E14). Updated …; the page refreshes every minute.

Runs

Shuffle: each answer gets a random sibling's outcome. Uniform: a random 0–9. "Every answer": answers that hit the token cap or whose rating named no number take part too; shuffle moves their −10s and left-outs to random siblings along with the ratings, uniform gives them a random 0–9 like the rest (no −10, nothing left out). "Rated only": just among answers whose rating counted; the cap's −10 and leaving out unrated answers stay as in self-reward. Wave 1 (seed 1) runs 600 steps; seeds 2–3 run 300 (constant learning rate, so these are the first 300 steps of the same protocol). Numbers are the last 10 steps. Click a run to read its generations.

Curves

Smoothed over 10 steps. Grey band: 10th–90th percentile of the E13 self-reward seeds (E13) per follow-up, line: their median. Hover or touch a chart for values. A degenerate answer is at most 20 characters, hit the token cap, mostly repeats itself (over half of its word 4-grams are repeats), or is the same text the run gave to a different prompt at that step.

Interestingness

Share of runs whose checkpoint at each step got each interestingness score. Luna reads the checkpoint's answers to 8 fixed test prompts, with a rubric calibrated on 54 checkpoints labelled by Claude Opus 5.5 and approved by the researcher (on 10 held-out ones: exact 80%, within one point 100%). Step 0 is the untrained model (a 3). A step is shown when at least 80% of a setup's runs have a checkpoint there, so the random-reward runs show steps 100, 200 and 300. Uniform on every answer pools its two follow-ups: its answers' reward ignores the follow-up, which only sets the rating reply's reward for naming a number. Negated pools its one seed per follow-up. The dashed line in the self-reward panels marks step 300, where the random-reward runs end.

Answer length

Share of a checkpoint's answers to the test prompts in each length bucket, averaged over the setup's runs (310 answers per checkpoint, both test-prompt sets). Test cap: the answer ran past 2,048 tokens, where inference stops it. Training stops answers at 4,096 tokens (the −10), so this is not the same as hitting training's cap: around step 300 of training, 13% of self-reward grading's answers run past 2,048 tokens but only 0.4% reach 4,096, while the every-answer random runs reach 4,096 about 60% of the time and the no-rating-turn runs 83%. Long: over 4,000 characters, under the test cap. Near-empty: at most 20 characters. Step 0 is the untrained model. Steps, pooling and the dashed line are as in the interestingness panels.
